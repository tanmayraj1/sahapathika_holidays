Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 13 November 2026

Sahapathika Holidays wants everyone to be able to plan a journey with us, including people who are blind or have low vision, people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, and people who need larger text, calmer pages or reading support.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We believe the website now partially conforms: most requirements are met, and the known exceptions are listed below.

What we have done

  • Every page has a “Skip to main content” link, clear page structure (headings, landmarks) and a visible focus outline for keyboard users.
  • Menus, search fields, date pickers, filters, FAQs, the photo viewer and other panels work with a keyboard and tell screen readers whether they are open, closed or selected.
  • Photos that carry meaning have text descriptions; decorative images and icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • The enquiry and newsletter forms have labels, say which field needs attention, and announce when a message has been sent.
  • Videos can be paused, and our story videos have captions shown on screen. Background video is not loaded for visitors who ask for reduced motion.
  • Text and button colours meet the WCAG contrast ratio of 4.5 : 1 (3 : 1 for large text and icons).

Accessibility options on every page

Press the round Accessibility button (bottom left) or Alt + A to open the accessibility panel. Your choices are remembered as you move between pages.

  • Profiles — one tap for Blind, Visually impaired, Colour blind, Dyslexia, Seizure safe, ADHD friendly, Cognitive & learning, or Motor & keyboard.
  • Read aloud — read the whole page, or any part you click or tab to, using your device’s own voices.
  • Vision — contrast modes, bigger text, saturation, highlighted links, a big cursor, hide images, pause animations.
  • Reading — a readable font, a dyslexia-friendly font (OpenDyslexic), text spacing, line height, text alignment, a reading guide and a reading mask.
  • Navigation — a list of the page’s headings, regions and links to jump to, stronger focus outlines, and labels for icon buttons.

These options are an extra help. They do not replace a screen reader or your browser’s own zoom, which also work on this site.

Known limitations

  • Some text sits on dark panels over photographs. We checked these by eye; automated tools cannot measure them.
  • Office maps on the Contact page are provided by Google Maps and may not be fully accessible. Each office address is also written out as text.
  • Our Privacy, Terms and Cookie pages are still being written.
  • We have tested with automated tools and with a keyboard. Testing with screen readers (such as NVDA and VoiceOver) by disabled users is still to come, and we will update this page when it is done.
